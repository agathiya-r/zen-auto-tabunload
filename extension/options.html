ZEN IDLE UNLOADER

Give your tabs a rest.

Idle pages unload from memory. Their tabs stay exactly where you left them and reload when selected.

minutes without use

Checked once a minute. Every browser restart begins a fresh timeout.

Always protected

Essentials, active tabs, playing audio or video (including muted video), picture-in-picture, and detected calls or screen sharing.

Ordinary pinned tabs and pages saved in bookmarks can unload. Internal, private and inaccessible pages are skipped.

Unloading reloads the page next time. The browser retains tab history and restores supported session state; arbitrary in-page work may not survive. Pages with a before-unload prompt remain protected by the browser.
